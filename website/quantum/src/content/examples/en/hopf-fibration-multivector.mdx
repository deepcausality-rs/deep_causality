The Hopf fibration maps the 3-sphere onto the 2-sphere, which is exactly the map
from a normalized two-component spinor to a point on the Bloch sphere. The
example encodes the state as a `HopfState` multivector, applies a fibre shift,
and projects.

The fibre shift is a global phase change. It moves the state along the fibre and
leaves the projected point where it was, which is the geometric statement of why
global phase is not observable. Anyone debugging a quantum control loop has hit
that fact from the other direction.

This example does not import `deep_causality_quantum`. `HopfState` lives in
`deep_causality_multivector`, alongside the `HilbertState` ket this crate builds
on.
