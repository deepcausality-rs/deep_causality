A global phase moves the state and leaves its Bloch vector fixed, and an experiment reads only the
Bloch vector. Over one full circuit of the fiber (8 steps, `θ` up to 4π), the state moves by
up to 4.000000 and its Bloch vector moves by 0.000000. Both displacements are squared Euclidean
distances in the ambient algebra.

A qubit state is a point on the 3-sphere `S³`, and the Bloch vector is a point on the 2-sphere `S²`.
The Hopf fibration maps `S³` to `S²`. Each point of `S²` is the image of a whole circle in `S³`, the
fiber, and moving along the fiber multiplies the state by a global phase.

## The Bloch vector never moves

```text
Walking the fiber from |+>, one complete circuit in 8 steps
  theta      state moved on S^3   shadow moved on S^2
   1.571 rad         0.585786            0.000000
   3.142 rad         2.000000            0.000000
   4.712 rad         3.414214            0.000000
   6.283 rad         4.000000            0.000000
   7.854 rad         3.414214            0.000000
   9.425 rad         2.000000            0.000000
  10.996 rad         0.585786            0.000000
  12.566 rad         0.000000            0.000000
```

Each row is one `fiber_shift` from `|+>`. The second column is the squared distance between the
starting rotor and the shifted rotor; the third is the squared distance between their Bloch
vectors. The state column reaches 4.000000 at `θ = 2π`, where the rotor equals its antipode −R, and
returns to 0.000000 at `θ = 4π`. `fiber_shift(θ)` applies the phase `θ/2`, so the rotor needs `4π`
to come back; this is the spinor double cover. The shadow column is 0.000000 in every row.

## A sandwich product computes the Bloch vector

`project` computes the Bloch vector as the sandwich product `R σ₃ R̃` of the state's rotor `R`,
so no coordinate formula appears anywhere. `HopfState` holds the state as that rotor in Cl(3), the
even subalgebra whose elements are the unit quaternions. Three tensor operations drive the run: `fmap` takes the fiber steps,
`zip_with` pairs each state with its own projection, and `fold` keeps the largest displacement in
each column. Those two maxima are the 4.000000 and the 0.000000.

Before the walk, the run projects the six cardinal states: `|0>` and `|1>` land on ±z, `|+>` and
`|−>` on ±x, `|+i>` and `|−i>` on ±y. A projection that exchanged two axes would put `|+>` on +y, so
the six rows pin all three axes.

## The run covers one qubit in pure states

The run covers one qubit in pure states, with no dynamics and no noise. Mixed states, where the
Bloch vector lies inside the ball, and the relative phase between qubits are outside it.

This example does not import `deep_causality_quantum`. `HopfState` lives in
`deep_causality_multivector`, beside the `HilbertState` ket that this crate builds on.
