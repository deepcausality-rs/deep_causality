Multiplying a state by a global phase moves it along a circle in the space of states and leaves
its projection onto the Bloch sphere where it was. The example walks one full circuit of that
circle. The state moves by up to 4.000000 and its shadow moves by 0.000000.

A qubit state is a point on the 3-sphere, and an experiment reads a point on the 2-sphere. The
Hopf fibration is the map between them. Each point of the 2-sphere is the image of a whole circle
in the 3-sphere, the fiber, and moving along the fiber is exactly a global phase.

## What you see

```text
Walking the fiber from |+>, one complete circuit in 8 steps
  theta      state moved on S^3   shadow moved on S^2
   1.571 rad         0.585786            0.000000
   3.142 rad         2.000000            0.000000
   4.712 rad         3.414214            0.000000
   6.283 rad         4.000000            0.000000
   7.854 rad         3.414214            0.000000
   9.425 rad         2.000000            0.000000
  10.996 rad         0.585786            0.000000
  12.566 rad         0.000000            0.000000
```

The state column peaks at 4 halfway round, where the rotor has reached −R, and returns to 0 only
at the end of the circuit. A spinor takes two turns to come back, and the shadow takes one.

## How the map is written

The state is a rotor in Cl(3), the even subalgebra whose elements are the unit quaternions. The
projection is the sandwich product `R σ₃ R̃`, so no coordinate formula for the Bloch vector
appears anywhere. The example first pins all three axes with the six cardinal states, so no two
of them can be exchanged unnoticed.

## Crates

This example does not import `deep_causality_quantum`. The state type `HopfState` lives in
`deep_causality_multivector`, beside the `HilbertState` ket that this crate builds on.
