Almost all of it. At a physical error rate of 1 in 100, the recovery leaves 0.1638 of the noise's
0.1639. The composite's measured residual is the part of the noise the recovery does not remove,
which is the part a distillation round exists to drive down.

The example builds the case Lorenz and Tull defer (their section 7.1) as two abstractions on the
hand-built [[4,2,2]] code. The first link is the noise on every physical qubit with probability
p. The second link is the code's ideal recovery.

## What you see

```text
[p = 0]
composition law in frobenius-on-choi: ε ≤ ‖τ₂‖_post · ε₁ + ‖τ₁‖_pre · ε₂ over 1 queries, holds
  io: ε₁ = 0.000e0, ε₂ = 3.283e-15, ‖τ₁‖_pre = 1.000e0, ‖τ₂‖_post = 2.000e0, bound = 3.283e-15, measured = 3.283e-15
    exact: without noise both links commute

[p = 1/100]
composition law in frobenius-on-choi: ε ≤ ‖τ₂‖_post · ε₁ + ‖τ₁‖_pre · ε₂ over 1 queries, holds
  io: ε₁ = 1.639e-1, ε₂ = 3.283e-15, ‖τ₁‖_pre = 1.000e0, ‖τ₂‖_post = 2.000e0, bound = 3.277e-1, measured = 1.638e-1
    the recovery leaves 1.638e-1 of the noise's 1.639e-1; the law allows 3.277e-1

[p = 5/100]
composition law in frobenius-on-choi: ε ≤ ‖τ₂‖_post · ε₁ + ‖τ₁‖_pre · ε₂ over 1 queries, holds
  io: ε₁ = 7.680e-1, ε₂ = 3.283e-15, ‖τ₁‖_pre = 1.000e0, ‖τ₂‖_post = 2.000e0, bound = 1.536e0, measured = 7.667e-1
    the recovery leaves 7.667e-1 of the noise's 7.680e-1; the law allows 1.536e0
...

Every law held, at every probability and every precision.
```

The program sweeps p over 0, 0.01 and 0.05 and repeats the p = 0.05 chain at `f32`, `f64`
and `Float106`.

## Read before you cite

This is an example with checks. It claims the residuals it measures and the bound the law
records. The source paper defers this case, so the example carries no theorem to compare against.
