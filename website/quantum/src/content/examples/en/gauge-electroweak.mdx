The run predicts a W boson mass of 80.369072 GeV against a measured 80.377000 GeV. The 7.928 MeV
difference is inside the 20 MeV tolerance the run sets. Tree level, `M_W = g·v/2`, gives 78.909302
GeV, which is 1.459770 GeV low.

The loop solver takes four measured inputs: the fine structure constant at the Z mass, the Fermi
constant, the Z mass and the top-quark mass (172.52 GeV). From them the run derives the couplings,
the W mass, the Z widths and the resonance cross-section. The tree-level gap is roughly two percent
of `M_W`; the one-loop radiative corrections close it, and the top quark in the loop dominates them.

## The loop correction closes a 1.46 GeV gap

```text
  M_W tree level        78.909302 GeV   from g*v/2
  M_W loop corrected    80.369072 GeV   from the loop solver
  the corrections move it by 1.459770 GeV
...
  M_W computed          80.369072 GeV
  M_W measured          80.377000 GeV   PDG
  deviation                 7.928 MeV   tolerance 20 MeV
  verdict            inside one-loop accuracy
```

The first three lines come from stage 2: the tree value, the loop-solver value and the 1.459770 GeV
between them. The last four come from stage 3. The deviation is the absolute difference between the
computed mass and the PDG value, and the verdict line reports whether that difference is below the
tolerance.

## The 20 MeV tolerance is the accuracy the verdict claims

The tolerance is 20 MeV, roughly the size of the two-loop terms that a one-loop calculation omits.
`within_one_loop_accuracy` returns true when the deviation is under it, so the verdict line tests
exactly the claim it makes. A looser tolerance would pass a calculation that had stopped agreeing
with the measurement.

## Four stages run as one flow

A `CausalFlow` chains the stages: unification (the couplings `g` and `g'`), symmetry breaking (the
masses), gauge mixing (the W/Z mass relation and the `ρ` parameter) and the Z resonance (widths and
peak cross-section). `bind_or_error` links them, so a stage that fails stops the later stages and
passes its error to the printed summary. The invisible width of the Z, 0.502289 GeV, is three neutrino
generations times one neutrino's partial width; its LEP measurement established that there are
three light neutrino generations.

## The deviation is one number with no error bar

The run carries no uncertainty on its inputs. The model uses the on-shell scheme with the library's
packaged one-loop corrections and treats the Z as a Breit-Wigner resonance at its peak. It contains
no two-loop corrections, no QED or QCD corrections to the widths, no initial-state radiation and no
fit over several observables.

This example does not import `deep_causality_quantum`. The formulas come from
`deep_causality_physics`, and `CausalFlow` comes from `deep_causality_core`.
