The example solves the implicit equation linking the Fermi constant to the gauge
boson masses,
`M_W²(1 − M_W²/M_Z²) = πα / (√2 G_F (1 − Δr))`, and reports the W mass and the ρ
parameter with one-loop radiative corrections included.

Two corrections carry the result. Veltman screening, `Δρ`, comes from the
top-bottom mass splitting and lands near 0.009. QED running, `Δα`, comes from
photon vacuum polarization. Leave either out and the prediction misses the
measured mass by far more than the roughly 0.02% the example achieves.

This example does not import `deep_causality_quantum`. The formulas come from
`deep_causality_physics`.
