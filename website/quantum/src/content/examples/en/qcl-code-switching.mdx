The detour costs the first link's residual, and the law turns that into a bound on the whole
switch. Switching codes is how a machine gets gates that one code cannot do transversally. The
price of the detour is a number, and the run prints it.

Two logical qubits are encoded into the hand-built [[4,2,2]] code, decoded, and encoded into the
[[8,2,2]] toric code, which then runs Z̄. The decode-and-re-encode is the switching gadget.

## The noisy switch leaves 0.4619 under a bound of 3.695

```text
[noiseless gadget] at FloatType
composition law in frobenius-on-choi: ε ≤ ‖τ₂‖_post · ε₁ + ‖τ₁‖_pre · ε₂ over 1 queries, holds
  io: ε₁ = 0.000e0, ε₂ = 0.000e0, ‖τ₁‖_pre = 1.000e0, ‖τ₂‖_post = 8.000e0, bound = 0.000e0, measured = 0.000e0
    exact: the gadget is the identity on the logical space

[gadget with depolarising noise p = 1/10 on one logical wire] at FloatType
composition law in frobenius-on-choi: ε ≤ ‖τ₂‖_post · ε₁ + ‖τ₁‖_pre · ε₂ over 1 queries, holds
  io: ε₁ = 4.619e-1, ε₂ = 0.000e0, ‖τ₁‖_pre = 1.000e0, ‖τ₂‖_post = 8.000e0, bound = 3.695e0, measured = 4.619e-1
    the noise is the first link's residual; code B's link stays exact; the composite
    sits under |t2|_post * e1 = 3.695e0
...
Outcome
  every composition law held    yes
  the noiseless switch exact    yes
```

With depolarising noise of probability 0.1 inside the gadget, the first link's residual is the
noise and the second link's stays zero. The composite's measured residual, 0.4619, lies under the
recorded bound, 3.695. The bound's first constant is the Frobenius-induced norm of code B's
recovery, which is 8.

## The bound is loose by a factor of eight

The run records that looseness: the measured residual, 0.4619, sits eight times under the bound,
3.695.
