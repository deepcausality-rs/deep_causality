The quantum geometric tensor carries both the geometry and the topology of a
family of quantum states over a parameter space. Its real symmetric part is the
quantum metric; its imaginary antisymmetric part is the Berry curvature. The
example computes both for twisted bilayer graphene, where the flat bands make
geometric contributions to transport dominant.

The velocity operator is `vᵢ = ∂_{kᵢ} H`, and the tensor sums over the bands
other than the one being tracked, weighted by the inverse square of the energy
gap.

This example does not import `deep_causality_quantum`. It builds on
`deep_causality_physics` for the band model and `deep_causality_tensor` for the
numerics.
