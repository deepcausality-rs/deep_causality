The Qi-Wu-Zhang Hamiltonian on a two-dimensional square lattice is
`H(k) = sin(kₓ)σₓ + sin(k_y)σ_y + (u + cos(kₓ) + cos(k_y))σ_z`. Its lower band
carries a Chern number, and that integer decides whether the material is a
trivial insulator or a topological one with conducting edge states.

The walk computes the Berry connection as the overlap of neighbouring
eigenstates, takes the Berry flux around each plaquette as `F = Im ln(U₁U₂U₃U₄)`,
and sums the flux over the whole Brillouin zone. Dividing by 2π has to land on
an integer, which is the check that the discretization is fine enough.

This example does not import `deep_causality_quantum`. The quadrature comes from
`deep_causality_calculus` and the complex arithmetic from
`deep_causality_num_complex`.
