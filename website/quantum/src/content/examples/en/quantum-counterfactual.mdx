A `QuantumHistory` of `HilbertState` vectors rides the state channel. A gate
injects a bit flip, taking the qubit from |0⟩ to |1⟩. Syndrome measurement then
reads `P(|1⟩) > 0.9` and flags the error. The bad state is popped from the
history, an X gate is applied, and the final state verifies at `P(|0⟩) = 0.98`.

The point of the walk is that error correction here is a property of monadic
state threading rather than of a special-purpose recovery API. Because every
step's state is on the channel, the history is inspectable, and rewinding is an
ordinary operation on it.

This example does not import `deep_causality_quantum`. The ket carrier
`HilbertState` lives in `deep_causality_multivector`, and the effect plumbing
comes from `deep_causality_core`, so the walk runs on those two directly.
