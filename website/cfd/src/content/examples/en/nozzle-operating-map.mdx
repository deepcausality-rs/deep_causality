## What the run printed

Each row is a 1-D compressible duct march over 128 cells in a converging–diverging
nozzle (inlet 2 m², throat 1 m², exit 2 m² over 1 m; 300 kPa / 500 K reservoir). A
gate is a pass or fail check on a result. `[tripwire]` means its bound was pinned
from this code's own output, so a pass shows no regression.

```text
  p_back/p0   M_exit   shock x [m]      Cf
      0.90    0.325       0.6562    0.242
      0.80    0.368       0.7656    0.277
      0.70    0.420       0.8516    0.316
      0.60    0.493       0.9297    0.365
      0.30    2.123         none    0.857
      0.10    2.123         none    1.254

analytic references: first critical p/p0 = 0.9372, exit-plane shock at p/p0 = 0.5134

--- nozzle operating map ---
  [PASS] [tripwire] choking: every choked row crosses Mach 1 at the throat
  [PASS] [tripwire] shock position: every internal-shock row matches the closed form
  [PASS] [tripwire] shock-free profiles: every shock-free row tracks the area-Mach relation
  [PASS] [tripwire] physical thrust: thrust coefficient finite and positive on every row
=== All gates passed: nozzle operating map. ===
```

As back pressure falls, the normal shock moves downstream through 0.656, 0.766,
0.852 and 0.930 m, then leaves the duct. The last two rows are shock-free
supersonic exits. Their exit Mach holds at 2.123 because the area ratio sets it once
the nozzle is fully supersonic, while the thrust coefficient still rises from 0.857
to 1.254. Measured exit Mach is 2.123 against the design value of 2.197, 3.4% low.

## How the march is checked

Two analytic references are closed-form and do not come from the march: the first
critical ratio (0.9372), below which the throat chokes, and the ratio (0.5134) at
which the shock reaches the exit plane. Four gates check the march against closed
forms, with bands measured at 128 cells:

- **Choking.** Every choked row crosses Mach 1 within eight cell widths of the
  throat.
- **Shock position.** Every internal-shock row lands within twelve cell widths of
  the closed-form position.
- **Shock-free profiles.** Interior Mach stays within 5 percent of the area-Mach
  relation.
- **Physical thrust.** The thrust coefficient is finite and positive on every row.

The six marches run concurrently under the `parallel` feature
(`deep_causality_par`), and the sweep returns rows in input order.

## More on the limits

Each shock station is a cell-resolution estimate. The map covers the design
envelope, not startup transients.
