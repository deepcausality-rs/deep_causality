## What the run printed

A cylindrical member sheds vortices at a frequency set by the flow speed. When that
frequency approaches a structural mode, the member is driven at resonance. The
margin is `|f_struct − f_shed| / f_struct`. At the fastest airspeed (1.20 m/s,
Re 160) the wake sheds at 114.5 Hz against a 150 Hz mode, a margin of 0.236 against
the 0.15 placard minimum.

```text
airspeed [m/s]   Re      St      f_shed [Hz]   margin
      0.75       100  0.1818        68.2     0.545
      0.90       120  0.1818        81.8     0.455
      1.05       140  0.1909       100.2     0.332
      1.20       160  0.1909       114.5     0.236
```

The member is `D = 2.0 mm`, and `f_struct = 150 Hz` is a stated demonstration value.
The margin falls at every step. Each step cuts it by 0.09 to 0.12, so one more step of
the same size would take it below the placard (extrapolated, not run).

A gate is a pass or fail check on a result. All three pass:

```text
[PASS] strouhal band: extracted St in [0.1818, 0.1909], validated band [0.16, 0.21] for this grid
[PASS] resonance margin: min |f_struct - f_shed| / f_struct = 0.236, placard minimum 0.15
[PASS] finite wake: 4 sweeps returned a finite, oscillating wake
=== All gates passed: vortex-shedding resonance margin. ===
```

## What runs per row

Each airspeed gets a full DEC incompressible Navier–Stokes march past the member. A
wake probe gives the shedding frequency, and `St = f·D/U`. Four marches run
concurrently under the `parallel` feature, bit-identical to the sequential run.

## What each gate checks

The Strouhal gate uses a band validated for this grid. The unconfined laminar
reference is about 0.164 at Re 100, rising to about 0.185 at Re 160 (Williamson, per
the example README). Blockage and the coarse grid push St upward, to 0.1818 at
Re 100, and the band is wider than the reference to record that shift.

The finite-wake gate requires oscillation, not just finiteness. A wake that decays
to steady still produces a finite signal, and the frequency extractor reports noise
as a frequency. The [cylinder verification](/validation/#dec-cylinder) records the
case: a staircase boundary at 16 cells per diameter yields a steady wake and a
printed Strouhal of 0.2444, taken from seventh-decimal noise.

## More on the limits
