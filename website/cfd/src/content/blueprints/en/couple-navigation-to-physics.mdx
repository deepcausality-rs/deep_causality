In the corridor example the navigation filter drifts while the plasma blocks GPS and recovers
when the signal returns. Its position error against the true vehicle position starts at 0.1823 m
and grows by dead reckoning (integrating the vehicle's own motion sensors) to 1.5637 m at the
peak passage and 2.2879 m at the exit. After the signal reacquires it falls to 0.2804 m. Gate (3)
of the run reports the first, second and last of these figures.

`Coupling::between_steps()` builds an ordered list of stages, each a `PhysicsStage`. The list
runs after every solver step. The navigation stage is one entry, placed after the stages it
reads and before the stage that clamps the command. Condensed from `corridor_coupling` in
`examples/avionics_examples/src/shared/world.rs`, with the constructor arguments and three
stages (`FreestreamFeeds`, `SuttonGravesLoads`, `WeatherTelemetry`) left out:

```rust
Coupling::between_steps()
    .then(VibrationalLagStage::new(/* ... */))
    .then(FiniteRateIonizationStage::new(/* ... */))
    .then(RegimeClassify::new(l_char, trigger))
    .then(BankSteeredLift::new(rho_ref, cda_over_m, l_over_d))
    .then(TruthGnss { noise_draw })
    .then(TrajectoryNav::new(q_diag, gnss_var, optical_var).with_imu(imu))
    .then(CommandedBank)
    .then(CyberneticCorrect::new(SafetyEnvelope::new(max_heat_flux, max_g_load, max_bank_rad)))
    .build()
```

The return type is `impl PhysicsStage<2, FloatType>`.

## The filter runs at the solver's step rate

`TrajectoryNav` runs one `ReentryNavEngine` predict and one error-state Kalman filter (ESKF)
correction per entry. It sits in the same list as the flow and chemistry stages, so the filter
advances at the solver's step rate and does not need a loop of its own.

## Stage order matters in two places

`TruthGnss` publishes the GPS fix that `TrajectoryNav` consumes on the same step. In the other
order the filter would read the previous step's fix.

`CyberneticCorrect` runs last, so it clamps what the guidance stage wrote. It carries the
`SafetyEnvelope` limits on heat flux, g-load and bank angle.

## Stages hand data through the coupled field

Stages communicate through `CoupledField`. `initial_field()` seeds the navigation engine that
`TrajectoryNav` then carries through the march. From `world.rs`:

```rust
field.set_nav(ReentryNavEngine::new(nav_r0, nav_v0, lift(EARTH_GM), filter));
```

The `ImuModel` passed to `.with_imu(imu)` scales the accelerometer bias away from the filter's
standard-day priors. The [weather example](/tutorial/stage-2-weather/) varies that departure
across six atmospheres and measures the effect on dead-reckoning drift.

## Adding a stage costs no indirection

`PhysicsStage` is implemented for `()` and for the pair `(A, B)`. The stack is a type known at
compile time, so stage calls are static dispatch. `powered_descent_coupling` in `world.rs` is
the same stack extended with the burn stages.
