At Mach 25 and about 71 km, the RAM-C II flight condition, the stagnation-line target computes
the whole chain and finds the plasma frequency 4.4 times above the communications band. From
`deep_causality_cfd/verification/qtt_ramc_stagline/baseline.txt`:

```text
Exact Rankine–Hugoniot post-shock state (the transported energy, no reconstruction):
  T_inf -> T2 ............ 250 K -> 8044 K
  density ratio ρ2/ρ1 ... 20.349
  velocity ratio u2/u1 .. 0.049
  pressure ratio p2/p1 .. 6.547e2
  post-shock n_tot ...... 2.645e22 m^-3

Stagnation-line blackout (Saha/Park-2T at the post-shock state):
  ionization fraction α .. 2.007e-5
  peak electron density .. 5.310e17 m^-3
  plasma frequency ω_p ... 4.111e10 rad/s
  blackout (ω_p > comms) . true (comms band 9.40e9 rad/s)
  relaxation-profile bond  2  (smooth post-shock zone, O(1) rank)
```

The chain closes. `α · n_tot` gives 5.310e17 m⁻³ against the reported `n_e`, and
`√(n_e e²/ε₀mₑ)` gives 4.111e10 rad/s against the reported `ω_p`. Both agree to better than
0.1%.

Five disciplines run in that chain. Each consumes the previous one's output through a named
scalar. The last two publish members of the `RegimeClass` value that `RegimeClassify` stores on
the field:

```text
discipline              model                          publishes
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
compressible flow       Rankine–Hugoniot jump          T_tr, n_tot, pressure_atm
      ↓
vibrational relaxation  Landau–Teller, Millikan–White  T_ve, T_a
      ↓
finite-rate chemistry   NASA RP-1232 three-channel     alpha, n_e
                        network
      ↓
plasma electrodynamics  ω_p = √(n_e e² / ε₀ mₑ)        plasma_frequency
      ↓
link availability       ω_p > comms_band_rad_s         gnss_denied
```

Downstream, navigation degrades while `gnss_denied` holds, and the guidance decision uses the
degraded state. [Couple navigation to the physics stack](/blueprints/couple-navigation-to-physics/)
covers those two.

## Four stages carry the chain

Condensed from `corridor_coupling` in `examples/avionics_examples/src/shared/world.rs`, with
the comments left out:

```rust
Coupling::between_steps()
    .then(
        VibrationalLagStage::new(
            lift(T_VE_INITIAL),
            lift(FALLBACK_PRESSURE_ATM),
            lift(REDUCED_MASS_AMU),
            lift(THETA_VIB),
            lift(SHEATH_PEAK_AGE_S),
        )
        .with_pressure_field("pressure_atm"),
    )
    .then(
        FiniteRateIonizationStage::new(lift(FALLBACK_N_TOT))
            .with_density_field("n_tot")
            .with_sheath_renewal(lift(SHEATH_PEAK_AGE_S)),
    )
    .then(FreestreamFeeds)
    .then(RegimeClassify::new(lift(L_CHAR), utils::trigger()))
```

`VibrationalLagStage` reads `T_tr` and an optional per-cell pressure field. It writes two
scalars: `T_ve`, the lagged vibrational-electron temperature, and `T_a = √(T_tr·T_ve)`, Park's
rate-controlling temperature. It relaxes from the frozen free-stream value over one residence
time and does not integrate the carried `T_ve` over the step. That models a sheath refreshed by
new gas at every step.

`FiniteRateIonizationStage` reads `T_tr` and `T_ve` and builds each channel's controlling
temperature itself: ionization at the geometric mean, dissociation at Park's `T_tr^0.7·T_ve^0.3`,
electron channels at `T_e = T_ve`. It writes `alpha` and `n_e`. The network is reversible. It
carries dissociative recombination as well as forward ionization, and recombination is what
ends the blackout.

`RegimeClassify` reads the peak of `n_e` and converts it to a plasma frequency and a denial flag.

Order matters. A stage placed before its input exists reads the previous step's value.

## A misspelled field name disables a stage

The field names have no declared list. Each is a `&'static str` field defaulted in the stage's
`new()`, with an opt-in override such as `with_pressure_field` or `with_density_field`. The
producer side is a bare literal in the carrier, in `compressible_march_run.rs`:

```rust
field.set_scalar("speed", speed);
field.set_scalar("T_tr", t_tr);
field.set_scalar("n_tot", n_tot);
field.set_scalar("pressure_atm", p_atm);
```

The field store is a list of name and value pairs searched linearly. Two consequences follow.

A missing name is a silent no-op. The vibrational stage and the classifier return without doing
anything when their input field is absent, so a misspelled name disables the stage without an
error.

A per-cell field of the wrong length is a hard error. The vibrational and ionization stages
return a `DimensionMismatch` unless the length equals the cell count or 1.

## The flow does not see the chemistry

Each step runs `advance`, which computes the next conserved state from the conserved state
alone. Then the coupling runs and changes only the `CoupledField`. The marched flow reads two
kinds of value back from the field: `truth_state`, the vehicle state that sets the inflow strip,
and, in the retropropulsion example, `commanded_throttle` and the plume geometry that set a
forcing mask. It reads no chemistry field. The coupling has no chemical energy sink, no
electron-pressure term and no density or energy feedback. The `EosStage` documentation records
that no shipped consumer reads the pressure it writes.

Trajectory and flow do form a closed loop. `BankSteeredLift` writes an aerodynamic force to the
field's force channel. `TruthGnss` advances the vehicle with that force and republishes
`truth_state`. Before the next step, the descent schedule evaluates the atmosphere at that
state and sets the inflow strip.

Because chemistry never modifies the flow, the plasma state has no influence on the vehicle's
true trajectory. It reaches the navigation filter through the GPS-denial flag, which gates the
filter's correction. **Blackout degrades the estimate, not the truth.**

The corridor's 17 bank branches show the size of the effect. The flow columns barely move while
the miss distance spans about a factor of 14.

| Column | Range across 17 branches |
|---|---|
| Peak heat flux (W/m²) | 2.232e6 to 2.240e6 |
| Thermal load (J/m²) | 1.459e7 to 1.460e7 |
| Peak n_e (m⁻³) | 6.224e19 to 6.309e19 |
| Miss distance | 2.070 m to 28.881 m |

The branches diverge by decision, not by flow. Over the branch continuation window, the bank
command changes the sampled atmosphere too little to alter the shock-layer chemistry by more
than 1.4 percent in peak `n_e`. The miss column measures distance from an aim point that the
setup places 20 m from the unsteered landing point, so its scale follows from that choice.

The [retropropulsion descent](/tutorial/stage-3-retropropulsion/) is the closest case to a two-way
chemistry coupling, and it stops short of one. Its `PlumeImprint` seam gives the marched layer
state realism, but in-flight drag authority is the cited A0 correlation, not a decrement
contracted from the evolved field. The [limits page](/boundaries/#srp) records the measurement
behind that choice.

## The blackout window comes from the chemistry

`gnss_denied` is not a schedule. `BlackoutTrigger::evaluate` maps the peak electron density
through the plasma-frequency kernel and denies the link when the frequency exceeds one
configured angular threshold, `comms_band_rad_s`. Both sides are in rad/s, so no 2π conversion
appears. The corridor records the transitions:

```text
regime -> slip (GNSS-available), Kn=0.07829109848665225
regime -> slip (GNSS-denied), Kn=0.012690837165407727
regime -> continuum (GNSS-denied), Kn=0.00993838892165156
regime -> continuum (GNSS-available), Kn=0.0002551442196046344
```

Two independent things change. The Knudsen number crosses the slip-continuum band as density
rises. The comms state moves in and out of denial as the sheath forms and recombines. The two
are not synchronized, and the classifier tracks them separately. See
[detect and act on a regime change](/blueprints/handle-regime-change/).

## Where the chemistry stops

The corridor's chemistry is a single-point closure with `T_e = T_ve` lumped, and there is no
spatially resolved reacting layer. The stagnation-line target adds the fitted shock interface
and the transit-age profile.

Reported peak `n_e` depends on the model. The closed-form Park two-temperature controller
lands 1.27 decades below the RAM-C II flight anchor of about 1e19 m⁻³, after the N₂–N₂
reduced-mass correction (`μ = 14.007`). The uncalibrated finite-rate network lands 0.35 decades
above it (about 2.25×), inside a ±0.70-decade band pinned from the measurement. The
[validation record](/validation/#qtt-ramc) has both figures. The `T_e = T_ve` lumping is worth
roughly 2×, and the documented chemistry-model spread is 2× to 5×.

γ = 1.1 is an effective-γ closure chosen so the post-shock temperature lands near 8000 K. A
perfect gas at γ = 1.4 over-predicts T₂ at roughly 30 000 K.
